# Cafe Nova Premium Restaurant Website

## Overview
Build a polished, mobile-first single-page website for Cafe Nova that quickly communicates the restaurant’s buffet offer, Rs. 1,999 price, daily hours, Faisalabad location, menu, ambiance, and reservation path.

## Visual Direction
- Warm old-money palette: deep espresso/charcoal, parchment, muted olive, and restrained antique gold accents.
- Editorial serif display type paired with a clean modern sans-serif for navigation and body copy.
- Cinematic, original imagery for the restaurant interior, buffet, dishes, desserts, tea, drinks, seating, and night ambiance.
- Full-screen photographic opening, elegant typography, generous spacing, restrained motion, and crisp high-contrast controls.
- Sticky navigation that becomes translucent on scroll, with a smooth mobile menu.

## Page Structure
1. Cinematic opening with Cafe Nova’s tagline, buffet positioning, two actions, and scroll cue.
2. Immediate information strip for rating, price, hours, and location.
3. Editorial “Nova Experience” story with floral-arch lounge imagery.
4. Dominant signature buffet offer with visible Rs. 1,999 and No GST messaging.
5. Readable tabbed menu containing exactly the supplied items.
6. Immersive editorial food and ambiance gallery with labeled image overlays.
7. Four concise reasons to visit.
8. Dramatic late-night dining feature.
9. Verified rating summary without invented testimonials.
10. Location details with a clearly labeled map placeholder and directions placeholder.
11. Reservation form prototype that acknowledges requests without claiming confirmation.
12. Contact area and premium dark footer using placeholders for unavailable phone/social links.

## Interactions
- Smooth anchor navigation and section reveals.
- Slow opening-image zoom, restrained parallax, image reveal masks, and subtle card/gallery hover effects.
- Horizontally scrollable menu categories on mobile.
- Accessible mobile navigation, thumb-friendly actions, readable menu text, and single-column reservation fields.
- Form validation plus a transparent “request recorded for prototype only” state; no booking confirmation.

## Technical Details
- Keep the experience on `/` as a purposeful long-scrolling restaurant site.
- Build the visual tokens in the global design system and use semantic color/font utilities throughout.
- Generate and store a cohesive set of restaurant and food imagery locally through the project asset flow.
- Add page-specific metadata for Cafe Nova.
- Avoid a live map because coordinates were not supplied; present an elegant non-interactive location panel instead.
- Use only supplied business facts and menu items; do not invent phone numbers, URLs, reviews, offers, or testimonials.
- Validate the finished page at desktop and mobile sizes, checking navigation, menu tabs, form behavior, layout, and overflow.
